The Agile Backlog Manager That Brings Focus To Your Work.

Taskist is an open-source tool for transforming chaotic task lists into a clear, prioritized, and actionable backlog. Seamlessly manage bugs, features, and change requests in one place.

Why Choose Taskist?

Unified Backlog

Stop jumping between issue trackers. Manage P0 bugs, emerging feature ideas, and technical debt all within a single, consistent workflow.

Clarity & Prioritization

Intuitive ranking and sorting ensure your team is always focused on the next most valuable item, keeping sprints efficient and goals clear.

Built for Open Source

Simple codebase, easy setup, and a focus on community contribution make Taskist a transparent and reliable tool for any open-source project.
